PELATIH AI DI PERANGKAT

PRIVAT SEJAK AWAL

• Ekspor datamu kapan saja

LAINNYA

**App name** (max 30)

**Full description** (max 4000)

Liftio is a fast, simple and private gym workout tracker. Log every set, watch your strength climb, and get advice from an AI coach that runs right on your phone.

LOG WORKOUTS FAST
• Record sets, reps and weight in seconds
• Rest timer that vibrates when your rest is over
• Full history of every session

READY-MADE TEMPLATES
• Push Day, Pull Day, Leg Day, Upper Body Strength and Full Body Express
• Build your own templates for your program

35 EXERCISES WITH PHOTOS AND GUIDES
• Chest, back, shoulders, arms, legs and core
• Step-by-step instructions and technique tips for every exercise

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Charts for training volume, strength progression and muscle group balance
• Workout frequency and monthly activity
• Automatic personal records: heaviest weight, best estimated 1RM, most reps and best set volume

ON-DEVICE AI COACH
• Advice based on your training history: when to take a recovery day, push/pull balance and volume trends
• A suggested workout for today
• Runs entirely on your phone; your conversations are never sent anywhere

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• All data stays on your phone and works offline
• Export your data any time

AND MORE
• Workout reminders on the days you choose
• Kilograms or pounds
• Light, dark, pure black or system theme
