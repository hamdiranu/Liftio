Liftio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · App: Liftio (com.liftio.app) · Developer: Hamdi Ranuharja

In short: Liftio stores everything on your phone. We don't collect, upload, sell or share your personal data, there are no ads, and there is no analytics or tracking.

Who we are

Liftio is a workout tracking app for Android developed by Hamdi Ranuharja. If you have questions about this policy, contact hamdiranuharjaa@gmail.com.

Information the app stores on your device

To work, Liftio saves the following in a private database on your phone. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

  • Profile: the name you enter, and optionally your age, height, body weight, training experience, goals, preferred training days and session length.
  • Profile photo (optional): if you pick one from your gallery, Liftio keeps a copy on your device to show in the app.
  • Training data: workouts, sets, reps, weights, templates, favorites and personal records.
  • AI coach conversations: the coach runs entirely on your device. Your messages and its replies are stored locally and are not sent to any server or AI service.
  • Settings: units, theme, reminder times and similar preferences.

What we collect and share

Nothing. Liftio does not require an account, and it does not send your personal data or training data to us or to anyone else. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools, and it does not use your device's advertising ID.

Permissions

  • Photos / media: only when you choose a profile photo, and only the photo you pick.
  • Notifications: only for the optional workout reminders, which are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback and the rest-timer alert.

Some permissions listed for the app on Google Play (such as internet access, camera or microphone) are included by the system libraries Liftio is built with. Liftio does not use the camera or microphone, and it does not use internet access to send your data anywhere.

Exporting your data

Settings → Export my data creates a copy of your workouts, templates and records and opens Android's share sheet. Where that copy goes is entirely your choice; Liftio does not receive it.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data on this device permanently deletes your profile, workouts, templates, records and coach conversations. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in Android settings) removes everything Liftio stored, including your preferences. Because no data is stored on our side, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Liftio is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that send data off your device (for example optional cloud backup with an account), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
